# District Register

## Design Rule

Districts are world bible targets. They do not create map objects, NPCs, shops, spawn rules, collision, walkability, doors, or transitions.

## Crown Plaza

Source:

- Trinity Convergence Center
- Tri-Ritual Assembly Hall
- Collective Memory Bank
- Sacred Triangulation Point

Akalynth form:

- central civic plaza
- public fountain or oath stone
- notice board
- first major meeting place
- central crossing between the three old orders

Future hooks:

- first city story hub
- public events
- faction introductions
- civic notices
