## First-City Puzzle Seeds

### Three Roads

The plaza has three old roads. Each road points to one order. This can become a tutorial for reading city structure.

### Nine Bells

The Emberworks may eventually have nine bells or hammer marks used in a trial puzzle.

### Thirteen Tablets

The Archive may eventually contain thirteen tablets, with one missing or sealed.

### Twenty-Four Watch Marks

The wall may eventually have twenty-four carved watch marks, one for each hour or old guard oath.
