# Akalynth World Bible V1

## Core Framing

High City is the first city of Akalynth.

Akalynth is the game and world name. High City is the first playable-city design target. VaultCore Prime is the old/source name preserved in deeper lore as what the ancient orders once called the city.

## Foundation

Akalynth is the game-world adaptation of VaultCore civilization mythology. The source material is not imported raw. Its cosmic, system, and execution language is translated into classic MMO-facing language:

- memory
- spirit
- will
- echo
- sigil
- chronicle
- oath
- shrine
- archive
- forge
- law

The world should feel old, civic, mysterious, and readable. Players should encounter the myth through places, signs, NPC flavor, ceremonies, puzzles, and quest hooks rather than through backend or automation vocabulary.

## The Three Old Orders

High City is shaped by three old orders.

### Dreamforged

The Dreamforged are the order of memory, pattern, vision, echo, and sigil. In game-facing lore, they are not a playable species by default. They are an order whose towers and sanctums preserve dreams, reflected memories, and strange repeating patterns.

Their player-facing tone:

> The Dreamforged built towers that echo memory. Walk too deep and the city repeats your steps before you take them.

### Flamebound

The Flamebound are the order of will, trial, forge, fire, transformation, and courage. They are the keepers of Emberworks, trial halls, forges, and ceremonies of remaking.

Their player-facing tone:

> The Flamebound burn old names from steel and flesh alike. Their trials remake cowards into champions, or ash.

### Codexborn

The Codexborn are the order of archive, law, oath, records, prophecy, and public memory. They keep tablets, ledgers, warnings, contracts, and chronicle halls.

Their player-facing tone:

> The Codexborn seal oaths into bronze tablets. Breaking one marks the soul with a visible sigil.

## First City Myth

High City was first known among the old orders as VaultCore Prime. Common people do not use that name now. They call it High City because it stands above the lower roads and old drains, holding its plaza, archives, forges, sanctums, and gate under one civic wall.

The old name survives in tablets, closed archive rooms, and order rites.

## Main City Structure

High City has four founding civic spaces:

- Dream Sanctums: memory, dream, reflection, pattern, vision
- Flame Temples / Emberworks: forge, trial, transformation, will
- Scroll Sanctums / Archive: records, law, prophecy, oath, chronicle
- Trinity Convergence / Crown Plaza: civic meeting center where the three orders overlap

The layout target remains:

1. Gate approach
2. City gate
3. Crown Plaza
4. District paths to Dream, Flame, Scroll, market, house, guard, and archive zones
5. Sewer / lower-city hint

## Sacred Numbers

The source sacred numbers should become puzzle and lore numerology, not mechanics in this lane.

- 1: unity, the city as one civic body
- 3: the three old orders
- 6: six faces, six trials, or six archive seals
- 7: seven old peoples or seven sealed roads
- 9: nine steps of trial or nine bells
- 12: calendar, civic months, archive cycle
- 13: hidden myths, sealed tablets, or forbidden story count
- 24: old watch hours, tower marks, or full city ring
- 27: deeper order rites or three-times-nine puzzle structure
- 42: joke, riddle, or archivist answer, not automatic canon law

## What This Bible Does Not Implement

This bible does not implement:

- playable factions
- race/species selection
- class selection
- faction reputation
- quests
- shops
- crafting
- rituals
- puzzle mechanics
- law systems
- prophecy systems
- door, house, or ownership systems
- city runtime map promotion

Those are future gates.
