## Canon Decision From Lore Bible

`docs/AKALYNTH_LORE_BIBLE.md` canonizes this statement:

> Akalynth is the world that remembers. High City is where its first stones were raised.

The same file also states that Akalynth is the world/app/site, High City is the first city, and runtime identifiers such as `azura.json` and `GateToAzura` remain pending migration.
