# High City Districts

## District Model

High City uses plain MMO civic districts adapted from VaultCore's first-city structure.

## Crown Plaza

Source inspiration:

- Trinity Convergence Center
- central public assembly
- collective memory / meeting space

Akalynth adaptation:

- public plaza
- fountain or central stone
- notice board
- benches and banners
- first meeting point after entry

Future possible systems:

- public announcements
- player gathering
- proof board
- city events

Not claimed:

- no real event system
- no social matching
- no proof verification change
