# High City World Design

## Canonical Framing

High City is the first city of Akalynth.

It is the first full civic space a player should understand after onboarding: a walled capital district with a gate, plaza, market, records hall, guard presence, housing, and undercity hints.

## Design Role

High City should become the first production city candidate after the visual and authority gates are ready. It should replace Azura as the first-city concept, but this lane does not perform that runtime replacement.

## Player-Facing Pitch

High City stands above the lower roads and old drains, built around a public plaza where newcomers first see the wider world open. Its walls are old, its records are older, and its market is where most players learn that Akalynth is not only a combat loop. It is a place of names, ownership, trade, warning signs, proof, and return.

## City Structure

Target high-level route:

1. Rookguard gate approach
2. High City gate
3. Crown Plaza
4. Market lane, house block, archive, guard hall
5. Sewer hint / lower-city access point

This matches the accepted visual block hierarchy:

- north / top: wall and gate
- center: plaza, fountain, circulation
- upper-left: civic / castle / meeting hall
- upper-right: market lane
- lower-left: walk-in house block
- lower-right: sewer / undercity hint

## Tone

High City should feel:

- civic
- old
- readable
- slightly vertical
- player-populated before it becomes system-heavy

High City should not feel:

- cosmic infrastructure
- generated ritual platform
- abstract automation diagram
- overloaded with implemented mechanics before review

## Narrative Anchors

Use these as flavor anchors, not mechanics:

- The Gate: entry from onboarding into city life.
- Crown Plaza: the public heart.
- The Notice Board: tasks, announcements, public warnings.
- The Archive: receipts, records, chronicle flavor.
- The Emberworks: blacksmith, repair, future crafting flavor.
- The Market Lane: merchants and player economy future.
- The House Steps: first ownership fantasy.
- The Drain Gate: sewer and lower-city future.

## Current Non-Claim

This document does not make High City a production map. Runtime still has existing map names and mechanics until a later promotion lane changes them.
