# AKALYNTH_DESIGN_PROVENANCE_ADOPTION_V1 — Claim Boundary

## Accepted narrow claim

Project direction accepted the Akalynth design-provenance governance contract
with a federated authority clarification. The adoption execution is limited to
the contract, this decision packet, and the two documentation indexes.

## What this lane establishes

- Civil governance retains authority over G1–G15, enforcement, amendment, and
  auditability.
- Design-provenance governance covers decision lineage, implementation lineage,
  observed-build evidence, conformance, conflict resolution, reconsideration,
  and the World Architect mandate.
- The lore bible retains authority over approved narrative lore within its
  domain.
- Cross-domain conflicts require explicit resolution.
- Canon hierarchy is evidentiary precedence, not automatic override.
