# Monetization Blueprint — What Money May Purchase (and What It Never Can)

> **Status:** Superseded design sketch (non-binding for v1). This is the pre-formalization draft; its rules were hardened into the binding [`MONETIZATION_CONSTITUTION.md`](./MONETIZATION_CONSTITUTION.md) (the authoritative source). Kept for design history — do not cite it for guidance; cite the Constitution.

> Alright. I’ll design this as a future-proof blueprint, not a commitment. Think of it as a sealed scroll you can open later.

See also: `docs/MONETIZATION_CONSTITUTION.md` (formal policy language).
See also: `docs/MONETIZATION_JUSTIFICATIONS.md` (required “not power” justification IDs).

## The Goal

Money may support the world — never dominate it.  
Fairness is preserved even under pressure.

---

## Prime Law (Non-Negotiable)

Money can never buy power, outcomes, or bypasses.  
It may only buy expression, memory, time-compression without advantage, or world support.

Everything below obeys this.

---

## Layer 0 — What Is Forever Forbidden 🚫

These are constitutionally non-purchasable:

- Combat power (damage, stats, win probability)
- Drop rates or loot quality
- Progression speed relative to other players
- Access to exclusive content that affects outcomes
- Cooldown reduction, friction reduction, or penalty removal
- Leaderboard position or ranking influence
- PvP advantages (direct or indirect)
- Anti-cheat immunity or enforcement leniency

If it affects who wins, who survives, or who dominates — money is locked out.

---

## Layer 1 — Purely Cosmetic Expression 🎭 (Safe Forever)

**Identity & Presence**

- Skins (character, mounts, companions)
- Animations (idle, emotes, spell visuals only)
- Titles, badges, marks (non-stat)
- Voice packs / sound themes
- UI themes (maps, frames, HUD style)

**Invariant**: Cosmetics must never:

- Obscure hitboxes
- Reduce visual clarity
- Mimic rare drops visually

---

## Layer 2 — Narrative & Memory Purchases 📜

This is where your system becomes unique.

Purchasable:

- Personal Chronicle Entries
- “Record this moment permanently”
- Legend Engraving
- Optional inscription attached to a real achievement
- Death Echoes
- Visual memorials when a character dies
- World Witness Marks
- Cosmetic markers showing participation in historic events

Important rule: You can only record what you actually did.  
Money buys preservation, not fabrication.

---

## Layer 3 — Time Convenience (Strictly Bounded) ⏳

This is the most dangerous layer — so it’s tightly constrained.

Allowed:

- Offline notifications / logs
- Inventory organization tools
- Replay tools (combat logs, heat graphs, receipts UI)
- Cosmetic fast-travel visuals (not shorter routes)

Proposed, **not yet ratified** into the binding constitution (the items above
mirror `MONETIZATION_CONSTITUTION.md` Article IV.3; this one does not appear there yet):

- Queue convenience (cosmetic priority, not faster completion) — requires a
  constitutional amendment before it is permitted. Until ratified, treat as not allowed.

Explicitly Not Allowed:

- Faster XP
- Reduced cooldowns
- Shortened timers
- Skipped challenges

Time saved must never convert into more progress per hour.

---

## Layer 4 — Social & World Support 💠

This is where money supports the ecosystem.

Purchasable:

- Guild banners & halls (visual scale only)
- Event sponsorship (cosmetic world changes during events)
- NPC patronage (flavor dialogue, appearance changes)
- Server-wide festivals (temporary visual/audio changes)
- Community-funded monuments

Key rule: Effects must be shared, not owned.

---

## Layer 5 — Services, Not Advantages 🛠️

These help operate, not win.

- Character rename / appearance reset
- Account transfers (with audit receipts)
- Extra character slots (with shared progression caps)
- Private practice instances (no rewards)
- Replay/sandbox simulations

---

## Crypto / Money Interface (Future-Safe Design)

If you ever use crypto or real money:

It should:

- Enter as receipted credits
- Never be tradable for power
- Be capped per period
- Be auditable and reversible at the policy layer

Ideal abstraction:

```text
Money → Support Tokens → Non-competitive uses only
```

No direct money → gold → power loops.

---

## Diablo 3 Failure — How This Blueprint Avoids It

| Diablo 3 mistake | Your design |
|---|---|
| Loot became purchasable | Loot is sacred & bound |
| Best play = market | Best play = play |
| Economy replaced gameplay | Economy is optional & peripheral |
| Power was monetized | Power is sealed |

---

## A Final Guiding Sentence (Worth Keeping)

Money may shape how the world looks, remembers, and celebrates — never how it decides.
